Every international consignment exceeding $250 is dispatched via our climate-controlled courier network with dedicated couriers in major metropolitan centers. Garments are transported in archival garment bags crafted from breathable Japanese cotton, while ceramic and delicate living objects travel in custom cedar casings. You receive real-time discreet SMS tracking and can schedule evening or weekend delivery windows.
